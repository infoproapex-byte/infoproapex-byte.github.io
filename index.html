How we work
🚪How we fit doors

Your door is measured on site, ordered to size and fitted by our team. We check it is level and square, seal it against draughts and water, fit the locks and handles, and test it before we leave.

Then we make good: we repair the brickwork, plaster and paint around the frame so the opening looks finished, not just filled.

🪟How we fit windows

We remove the old window carefully, prepare the opening, then fit the new frame level and secure with proper packers, fixings and sealing. Every opening is checked, and the window is tested before we finish.

After fitting we repair the brickwork and reveals, plaster and decorate around the window, then clean up.

🎨How we paint your walls

We use VOC-free paint: practically no smell and no harsh fumes.

  • A good choice for homes with asthma sufferers, allergy sufferers and children.
  • We can paint in winter, because there is no strong smell, so you don’t need to open every window and let the cold in.
  • Walls are prepared properly: filled, sanded and primed where needed, then given a clean, even finish.
🧱Repairs and finishing

After every fitting we repair what the job disturbed: brickwork, plaster, paint, sockets and switches. When we leave, the job is finished. You don’t need another tradesperson.

📝Contract, price and deposit
  • The price you see in the catalogue includes fitting. You pay only what you see, with no hidden extras.
  • Every job is covered by a written contract, read and approved by both sides.
  • The deposit, as agreed in the contract, is paid when we bring the door, windows or furniture. We don’t bring anything into your home before the deposit is received.
