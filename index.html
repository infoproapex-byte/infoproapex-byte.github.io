How a job runs

From your first enquiry to the final check, we agree the installation scope with you.

  1. Discuss

    Share your plans, product details and preferred installation dates.

  2. Survey

    I check the room, measurements and plans before any work begins.

  3. Prepare

    Agree site preparation and protect the working area.

  4. Fit

    Units are levelled, secured and aligned, then worktops and appliances go in.

  5. Hand over

    A full walk-through together, and the space left clean and tidy.

  6. Aftercare

    Discuss any installation concerns with us after completion.
