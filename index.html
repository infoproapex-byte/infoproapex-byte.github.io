12-Month Workmanship Guarantee

All installations come with a 12-month workmanship guarantee from the date of completion. We will remedy defects caused by our installation work at no additional charge.

This guarantee covers workmanship, not the customer-supplied products themselves. It excludes manufacturing defects, accidental damage, misuse, normal wear and tear, and faults caused by subsequent alterations by others.

To make a claim, contact us at infoproapex@gmail.com or call 07775 877746.

Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.
