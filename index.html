How a job runs

Your design comes from a Wren Kitchens designer, and my team fits it. You know what is happening and when, from first call to final check.

  1. Design

    A Wren Kitchens designer plans your kitchen or bedroom with you and agrees the layout.

  2. Survey

    I check the room, measurements and plans before any work begins.

  3. Prepare

    Floors and walls are protected and the old kitchen is removed safely.

  4. Fit

    Units are levelled, secured and aligned, then worktops and appliances go in.

  5. Hand over

    A full walk-through together, and the space left clean and tidy.

  6. Aftercare

    If anything needs adjusting after the fit, I come back and put it right.
